## Hidden axioms and degrees of freedom

1. The four chart coordinates, chart identity, rank-one hidden state, latent AR
   invariance, loading family, and `tanh(source)` basis are known by design.
2. Paired interventions share hidden state and process noise and supply 512
   additional probe pairs.  Observation-only models are not equal-information
   controls; only the same-probe dense arm is.
3. Validation/test uncertainty is conditional on one frozen train/probe fit.
   It does not include learner retraining variance.
4. V1--V5 preregistration timing is asserted in files but not independently
   certified by Git, because registration, code, and results first appear
   together.  V6 alone is demonstrably pre-implementation.
5. Historical hashes depend on LF/CRLF serialization.  V7 must define a
   canonical newline/JSON hash rule in addition to preserving raw provenance.
6. All synthetic coordinates must be declared dimensionless or normalized by
   training-only chart scales before a shared RMSE is formed.
