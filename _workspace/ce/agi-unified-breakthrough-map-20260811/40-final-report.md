# Unified breakthrough direction

Status: COMPLETE

The selected direction is one modular **Adaptive Causal Belief-State Model**,
not a collection of independently tested V9 candidates.

Its structural spine is the surviving sparse causal mechanism. Its actual new
capability is a prefix-inferred, two-timescale low-rank residual belief with a
posterior covariance. The belief correction enters each rollout transition,
and uncertainty controls trust in that correction. This directly targets the
single-state residual bottleneck exposed by V8 while preserving the leakage,
symmetry, and stability discipline already established.

The architecture has reserved interfaces for regime belief, change-point
reset, episodic priors, edge-strength adaptation, hypothesis beams, and
planning, but they are disabled in the first model. This keeps all ideas on
one direction while ensuring the first result remains attributable.

No V9 registration or execution should occur until this model, as one frozen
development configuration, demonstrates a material V5 advantage on a fresh
development block. The immediate next action is design-to-code of the core
state observer only, not another confirmatory test.
