# Alternative closure routes — dark-sector derivation

Status: COMPLETE

The P0 findings in `11-math.md` cannot be repaired by selecting a different central value. Each route supplies missing physical structure, free-parameter classes, and an independent falsifier. Every route becomes target-aware if its choices are made after inspecting an abundance or expansion endpoint.

## R1 — microscopic monitored, energy-complete neighbour model

Specify a finite-range system--bath Hamiltonian, bath state, seed, detectors/record algebra, and weak-coupling/Markov/secular limit. Derive the facilitated jumps, instrument and energy current, including compensating bath/drive stress. Test a coherent-Hamiltonian control, zero-drive energy control, and alternative unravellings of the same master equation.

Minimum free structure: Hamiltonian/couplings, bath state, detector/coarse graining, seed, locality scale (five classes). Falsify on total-energy failure, failure of the claimed diagonal limit, or record-dependent predictions without a selection principle. Success establishes a facilitation process only, not cosmological abundance.

## R2 — controlled infinite-volume or age-structured limit

Define graph sequence, parent lifetime law, generation/window rule, seed law and scaling. Prove a coupling/error bound from the exclusion process to either multitype Galton--Watson or Crump--Mode--Jagers branching. Use matched acyclic, cyclic-subcritical, high-collision, and growing-finite-volume controls.

Minimum free structure: graph sequence, scaling, time window/age kernel, seed law, error metric. Falsify if collision/common-bath errors do not vanish, exponential-lifetime variance violates the assumed offspring law, or survival disappears in the named limit. The Perron threshold then remains a conditional stochastic result, not a DM/DE result.

## R3 — covariant record-to-residual matching

Starting from R1/R2 records, derive a local covariant kernel for C1, conversion scale `M_*`, transition hypersurface, source current, compensating stress, and no-double-counting rule. Require well-posed metric variation and `nabla(T_visible+T_res+T_env)=0`. The ordinary two-outcome conditioning calculation is a mandatory negative control; an unrecorded standard-QM alternative cannot be relabelled as the new source.

Minimum free structure: kernel family, `M_*`, matching clock/surface, current, record coarse graining, source rule (six classes). Falsify on failure of locality/covariance/conservation or under physically equivalent unravellings. Success would derive C1 but would still not fix mass, amplitude, or vacuum offset.

## R4 — preregistered residual-scalar cosmology

Fix the scalar action, `m`, initial data, `V_Lambda`, residual fraction, radiation/neutrino assumptions, and transfer/visibility/nuisance choices before the tested likelihood. Evolve scalar perturbations in an Einstein--Boltzmann calculation against a frozen joint covariance. Keep `w=-1` in the constant-offset branch; a post-data CPL potential is a new fitted model.

Free parameters include at least `m`, amplitude/phase or initial distribution, `V_Lambda`, fraction, plus standard cosmological/nuisance parameters. Falsifiers are frozen distance data, CMB/lensing, Jeans/small-scale suppression and joint likelihood. Any same-data calibration must be reported as model fitting, not prediction.

## R5 — independent abundance mechanism

To overcome DSO-7, derive a conserved production/transfer action fixing yield, scalar amplitude and vacuum scale from independently measured microphysics. It must state species/current, reheating or transition history, entropy/energy transfer and observer epoch. `q=Omega_b`, `1-q=Omega_dark`, `alpha_s D=Omega_c/Omega_DE`, or a flatness complement are not substitutes.

Minimum free structure: production coupling/rate, mass scales, initial/reheating state, charge/current, transfer efficiency and vacuum-selection mechanism. Falsify through BBN/entropy/isocurvature, species abundance, fifth-force/decay, or cosmological transfer constraints. This is the only route class capable of generating an absolute split; CE has not supplied it.

## Decision

No closure route is complete. R1--R3 are required before “unselected paths source residual gravity” is a derivation. R4 is required for a scalar observational test. R5 is separately required for an absolute DM/DE split. Historical readouts, central-value matches and fitted scale factors cannot replace any of these routes.
