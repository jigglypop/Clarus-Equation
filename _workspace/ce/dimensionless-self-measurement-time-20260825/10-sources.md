## Source verdict

The literature supports repeated-to-continuous interactions, instrument semigroups, stochastic records and past-record feedback. It supports interpreting “self-measurement” operationally as a subsystem or later time slice responding to its own earlier record. It does not support an unpartitioned system obtaining a perfect classical copy of its whole unknown state.

The proposed

$$
\theta=-\ln(1-\eta)
$$

is therefore a model-specific semigroup coordinate for one fixed dephasing partition. The sources do not make it physical time, a new 0D spatial dimension, energy, consciousness, or a dark-sector source.

Trace distance supplies a defensible metric for the new “self differs from its
earlier self” statement, and Markov contraction supplies one possible arrow.
However, a nonzero local path speed alone does not imply irreversibility: periodic
unitary paths can move at every local step and return exactly to their initial state.
The source-supported name is therefore a record-dependent distinguishability flow,
not a proof that all physical time is self-measurement.
