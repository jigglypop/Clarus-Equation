# BA-TR12 final report

Status: `CURVATURE_ASSOCIATION_NOT_SUFFICIENT / ENDPOINT_CLOSED`.

Intrinsic curvature cannot be a generally sufficient route-cost statistic:
two flat nonlinear charts with the same small-signal metric have different
finite-amplitude saturation. Nevertheless, within the frozen BA-TR10 learned
rotation family, the preregistered small-amplitude absolute-curvature cost was
a substantially better held-out route surrogate than the preregistered metric
strain summary.

This licenses only a restricted predictive association. It does not make
curvature the stored memory, a causal force, a general optimal router, a
biological observable, or a physical energy. The next test must use fresh
rotations, interleaved directions, and a larger held-out amplitude without
changing the cost, then either confirm or stop the selector claim.
