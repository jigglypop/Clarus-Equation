# Primary-source audit: measurement duration, locality, and record dimension

Status: COMPLETE

Scope: primary or foundational papers only. This source lane tests whether measurement must be instantaneous and whether a discrete outcome can be treated as an additional physical spacetime dimension. It does not establish a cosmological identity for dark matter or dark energy.

## Boundary needed for the proposed 0D idea

The following quantities must not be conflated:

1. **Outcome/record space:** a measurable space $(\Omega,\Sigma)$. A single discrete outcome $r\in\Omega$ is an atom of record space and may be called operationally “0D” in the sense of having no continuous coordinates.
2. **Spacetime support:** a region \(\mathcal R_M\subset M\), which may be a point, worldline segment, worldtube, or bounded finite-volume region. Fewster–Verch specifically use bounded spacetime coupling regions.
3. **Protocol time support:** an interval \([t_0,t_1]\) or a continuous monitoring history. Continuous-measurement papers support nonzero temporal support and record histories.
4. **State-update map:** an instrument \(\mathcal I\), which maps input states to outcome-indexed subnormalized output states. Its mathematical outcome label is not a coordinate of spacetime.

Thus the defensible statement is:

$$
\dim(\Omega\text{ outcome atom})=0
\quad\not\Rightarrow\quad
\dim(M\text{ extra direction})=0.
$$

The literature supports a model in which a 0D record is produced by a finite-duration, local system–environment coupling. It does not by itself support promoting the record label to a physical extra dimension or assigning it energy/stress-energy. Those are additional dynamical hypotheses requiring a field equation, conservation law, and observational test.

## Relevance to a finite-duration “measurement wall”

A useful phenomenological witness, not a theorem from the sources, is

$$
\dot\rho_t=\gamma(t)\bigl(\mathcal D_P-I\bigr)\rho_t,
\qquad
\mathcal D_P(\rho)=\sum_rP_r\rho P_r,
$$

with accumulated wall strength

$$
\eta(t)=1-\exp\!\left[-\int_{t_0}^{t}\gamma(s)\,ds\right].
$$

For finite integrated rate, $0<\eta<1$ is a partial wall; the hard projective limit is a separate idealization. This distinction is consistent with the instrument, weak-measurement, continual-measurement, and local-QFT sources above, but the specific equation is a proposed model choice rather than an empirical result established by any one cited paper.

## Source conclusion

The primary-source audit supports the following narrow conclusion: “measurement” need not be a single instantaneous event, and a measurement record can have temporal history generated by a local finite spacetime interaction. It does not support the stronger conclusion that the record is a new physical dimension, that all unselected outcomes carry energy, or that such records are automatically dark matter/dark energy.
