# Research contract

Status: COMPLETE

## Objective

Treat every defensible V7 success as a frozen checkpoint and identify the next
algorithmic mechanism that can address only the remaining H20 bottlenecks.  The
target is not an AGI claim.  It is a development-stage successor route for the
same fully observed four-chart synthetic forecasting family.

## Frozen checkpoints

The next route must preserve all of the following unless a new preregistration
explicitly demonstrates a strictly stronger replacement:

1. Prefix-only prediction from `x[0:81]`, one origin, one uninterrupted H20
   rollout, no target-window updates, no hidden-state access, and instrumented
   maximum read index 80.
2. Training-only coordinate normalization and seed-level paired inference.
3. V7's positive sparse contribution checkpoint:
   `no_sparse - sparse = +0.020552`, paired 95% interval
   `[+0.008174, +0.032930]` on the disclosed V7 validation set.
4. Symmetric equal-probe dense and no-sparse controls, finite component paths,
   convex controller accounting, immutable registrations, and locked tests.
5. Preservation of all V1--V7 failures and unopened historical test splits.

These are engineering and synthetic empirical checkpoints, not evidence of
AGI, open-world causal discovery, brain equivalence, or physical CE closure.

## Remaining bottlenecks

On the disclosed V7 validation set the successor must explain, rather than
hide, three failures:

1. The consensus degraded its own V5 sparse parent:
   `V5 - sparse = -0.006491`, 95% interval
   `[-0.027431, +0.014449]`.
2. Persistence superiority was not established:
   `persistence - sparse = +0.010492`, 95% interval
   `[-0.016487, +0.037470]`.
3. Maximum dynamic-component pathwise Jacobian radius was `1.114309`, above
   the locked `0.98` bound.

## Research question

Can one structurally new, prefix-fitted controller or dynamics modification
retain the sparse checkpoint while making the V5 parent a safe anchor and
enforcing pathwise stability, without using V7 validation outcomes as new
confirmatory evidence?

## Development rules

- V7 validation is now disclosed development data only.
- V7 locked test seeds remain unopened and may never be reused as successor
  development data.
- Generate at least three structurally distinct candidates and count their
  tuning degrees of freedom.
- Prefer a mechanism with an analytic safety property over a threshold tuned
  to V7 outcomes.
- No successor version number, fresh validation seed block, or pass gate is
  assigned until the algorithm and all hyperparameters are frozen.
- This run may recommend a pilot implementation, but it does not authorize a
  confirmatory run or test opening.

## Deliverables

- Independent evidence, mathematical, and alternative-route lanes.
- A formal checkpoint/status audit.
- One ranked algorithmic route with explicit kill tests and a minimal pilot
  plan, or a documented blocker if no route preserves the checkpoints.
