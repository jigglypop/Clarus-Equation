## 1. Scope and evidence rule

This lane audited repository-primary evidence for the synthetic V1--V7 sparse
causal bridge lineage.  It did not evaluate physics, neuroscience, or external
AGI evidence, and it did not inspect or execute any locked test split.

- Audited current worktree: `main` at
  `fcb754ee5b1f35324e9502d3b3f146387eb25823`, with pre-existing staged and
  untracked research files.
- Historical immutable source line: branch `itself`, principally commits
  `5db40b9404cd385dbd8f0ce28a7365d166a73c82` (V1--V4) and
  `33836b89855d86c73e2ddf271b2c5eee6e1191b3` (V5--V6).
- V7 registration, implementation, test definition, validation artifact, and
  closure document are untracked in the audited `main` worktree.  Their
  internal hashes match one another, but Git does not yet provide immutable
  chronology for V7.
- `PASS` below means only that a stored **validation** artifact reports all of
  its registered checks true.  A partial true check inside a failed conjunction
  is called a development checkpoint, not a passed endpoint.
- Locked-test status and contents are deliberately `NOT INSPECTED` in this
  lane.  No test seed was simulated, read, summarized, or reused.

External evidence is **OUT OF SCOPE**.  The audited quantities are generated by
repository code in a four-chart synthetic family, so no web/current physics
baseline is needed for this source lane.

## 5. Constraints a successor must preserve

1. **Do not mutate V1--V7 evidence.** Add a new implementation/registration
   family; preserve V1, V3, V5, and V7 failures and V6's non-run status.
2. **Keep the honest forecast contract.** Input is immutable `x[0:81]`; origin
   is 80; generate one uninterrupted H20 path; H5 is only `H20[0:5]`; no
   target-window update, hidden input, oracle branch in the candidate, or
   future observation read is allowed.
3. **Keep training-only normalization and seed-level inference.** Scales must
   be fit only from inherited training states. The independent statistical
   unit remains one seed, with paired intervals computed on seed RMSE.
4. **Keep fair controls and budgets.** Retain persistence, the unmodified V5
   sparse parent, stable adaptive dense, no-sparse controller, and symmetric
   equal-probe dense controller under the same prefix, probe budget,
   normalization, and refit rule.
5. **Treat the V7 sparse subcheck as development regression only.** Preserve
   `+0.020552` and its positive paired lower bound when replaying disclosed V7
   validation, but never count that replay as successor confirmatory evidence.
6. **Protect the parent explicitly.** A new controller must expose an auditable
   V5 fallback/anchor and a kill test against parent degradation. Nonnegative
   weights summing to one are insufficient by themselves.
7. **Make stability structural and attributable.** Preserve or strengthen the
   `0.98` pathwise dynamic bound, record every seed/component radius, and reject
   any route that hides an unstable component behind a finite consensus.
8. **Use fresh evidence only after the algorithm is frozen.** Do not inspect or
   reuse any historical locked test split, especially V7's unopened split.
   V7 validation is development data; no new version/pass gate should exist
   until mechanism and hyperparameters are frozen.
9. **Improve provenance.** Commit/hash the successor registration before its
   implementation and result. Pin serialization/newline policy and preferably
   the executable environment, not only Python/NumPy version strings.
10. **Retain the narrow claim boundary.** Even a future pass supports only the
    registered synthetic conditional-forecast mechanism. It is not evidence of
    AGI, general causal discovery, brain equivalence, consciousness, or a
    physical CE mapping.
