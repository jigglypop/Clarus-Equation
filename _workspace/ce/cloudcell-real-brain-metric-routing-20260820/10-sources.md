## Hallinen et al. / PredictionCode (DPR3H)

- **Primary paper:** Kelsey M. Hallinen et al., “Decoding locomotion from population neural activity in moving *C. elegans*,” eLife 10:e66135 (2021), DOI [10.7554/eLife.66135](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.66135).
- **Official data:** Open Science Framework DOI [10.17605/OSF.IO/DPR3H](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/DPR3H). The paper states that the repository contains calcium activity traces and animal pose/position for all experiments.
- **Official analysis code:** [leiferlab/PredictionCode](https://github.com/leiferlab/PredictionCode), cited by the paper. The code is analysis code, not the data archive.
- **Organisms/strains:** *Caenorhabditis elegans*; AML310 and AML32 GCaMP6s calcium-imaging strains, with AML18 GFP control animals. AML310 includes an AVA label; the paper reports 11 calcium-recorded individuals and 11 GFP controls for the relevant comparison.
- **Modalities and alignment:** Whole-head population calcium imaging during free crawling on agar, with simultaneous posture/locomotion video. The methods report body imaging at 50 frames/s, fluorescence acquisition at 200 optical slices/s, and approximately 6 head volumes/s. Recorded variables include calcium traces, velocity, body curvature, position, and pose. One experiment records the same animal before and after levamisole immobilization, enabling within-recording moving/immobile comparison.
- **Neuron registration/anatomy:** Neuronal nuclear tag-RFP supports segmentation; AVA is additionally labeled in AML310. This paper is not a NeuroPAL all-neuron identity atlas, so identity-preserving cross-animal or long-term same-cell registration must not be assumed from the paper or OSF citation alone.
- **Perturbation:** Levamisole immobilization is a state manipulation in the same worm. It is not a neuron-specific causal intervention. The authors explicitly state that decoding alone cannot distinguish locomotion-driving signals from proprioceptive/monitoring signals and that further perturbation is needed.
- **Access/licence:** OSF is publicly linked by the primary paper. The publication is openly available under the eLife/CC-BY publication terms; the dataset’s exact OSF file manifest and reuse licence must still be checked before redistribution. A local cache is present under `data/external/cloudcell/`: `AML18_moving.tar.gz`, `AML32_moving.tar.gz`, `AML310_moving.tar.gz`, the `extracted/` sessions, and a local `PredictionCode/` checkout. The three local archive hashes and extraction/schema state are recorded in `artifacts/cloudcell-input-audit.md` and independently regenerated in `artifacts/cloudcell-input-audit.json`. The original OSF object/version and acquisition receipt remain incomplete.
- **Empirical boundary:** Suitable for re-computing within-recording population correlation/state-space changes and behavior-aligned prediction. It does not by itself establish directed causal routing, a Riemannian metric, or a mammalian-brain mechanism.

## Randi et al. / functional propagation atlas (E2SYT)

- **Primary paper:** Francesco Randi et al., “Neural signal propagation atlas of *Caenorhabditis elegans*,” Nature (2023), DOI [10.1038/s41586-023-06683-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06683-4).
- **Official data:** Machine-readable measurements are deposited at Open Science Framework DOI [10.17605/OSF.IO/E2SYT](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/E2SYT). The paper also links the interactive atlas at [funconn.princeton.edu](https://funconn.princeton.edu) and [funsim.princeton.edu](https://funsim.princeton.edu).
- **Official code:** [leiferlab/pumpprobe](https://github.com/leiferlab/pumpprobe), together with the linked worm data/segmentation/atlas repositories named in the paper.
- **Organisms/strains:** *C. elegans* TWISP strain AML462/related functional-connectivity strains. The transgene combines nuclear GCaMP6s, GUR-3/PRDX-2 optogenetic activation, and NeuroPAL fluorophores. An unc-31 knockout version is used to reduce dense-core-vesicle signalling in a control condition.
- **Modalities and alignment:** Awake but immobilized animals; whole-brain cellular-resolution calcium imaging at approximately 2 volumes/s, with targeted two-photon stimulation. Stimuli are generally delivered every 30 s, with 300–500 ms pulse trains; target and downstream calcium traces are recorded in the same trial/time base.
- **Neuron registration/anatomy:** NeuroPAL multichannel colours plus position/size are used for cell identity. Nonrigid point-set registration tracks neurons across volumes. The paper notes that some dim/crowded neurons are difficult to identify and are absent or less frequent, so missingness and identity uncertainty must be retained in any reanalysis.
- **Perturbation:** Direct single-neuron optogenetic activation; pulse duration/power and target neuron are recorded. The paper reports functional propagation across 23,427 neuron pairs and compares wild-type signalling with an unc-31 mutant background. This is the strongest of the two sources for an intervention-defined directed edge, but it is not a freely moving behavioral dataset.
- **Access/licence:** OSF E2SYT is the official public data route cited by the primary paper. Exact OSF manifest, byte sizes, checksums, and dataset-specific reuse licence were not retrieved here. No local E2SYT object, checksum, or acquisition receipt exists in this run.
- **Empirical boundary:** Suitable for measuring intervention-conditioned target→response maps, response latency/amplitude, functional-versus-anatomical edge comparisons, and covariance/state changes around stimulation. It does not establish a mammalian cortical/PFC mechanism or a unique Riemannian metric.
