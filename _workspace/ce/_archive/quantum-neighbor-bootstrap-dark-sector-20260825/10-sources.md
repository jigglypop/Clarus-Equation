## Source-grounded conclusions and no-go constraints

1. **Conditional neighbor triggering is standard.** Harris’ classical contact process and facilitated quantum-spin models both use local rates constrained by neighboring activity. Thus the candidate jump operator is a recognizable conditional-dynamics ansatz, subject to complete positivity and a declared microscopic realization.
2. **A cycle/SCC is not a survival theorem.** It can establish mutual reachability, but extinction/survival additionally depends on rates, losses, offspring law, collisions, finite capacity, graph geometry, and the limiting procedure. In a finite network with an accessible vacuum, absorption occurs under the usual irreducibility assumptions; long lifetime is metastability, not infinite-time persistence.
3. **The Perron threshold is conditional.** \(\rho(A)>1\) is a theorem for the specified multitype branching process, not for the original interacting Lindblad network. Deriving \(A\) requires a generation window and control of overlap, exclusion, depletion, parent lifetimes, and correlations.
4. **Bootstrap needs a seed and an energy ledger.** If all nodes are inactive and every raising jump is multiplied by an inactive-neighbor occupation, the vacuum is absorbing. Neighbor dependence supplies a gate, not energy. Sustained activation requires an initial seed and bath/drive/Hamiltonian work accounting.
5. **Quantum measurement/decoherence does not close the CE bridge.** An unraveling supplies conditional records only after an instrument and system–environment split are chosen. The literature supports the need for this extra structure; it does not identify discarded outcomes with a gravitating residual field.
6. **Dark-sector predecessor remains an axiom/map.** The source record supports the local facilitated network and the conditional stochastic reductions only. The map from unselected histories to a covariant residual stress tensor, and its split into dark matter-like and dark-energy-like components, remains a separate CE physical-map axiom followed by conditional EFT theorems. No cited source supplies the observed abundance or equation-of-state split.

## Explicitly unverified / not claimed

- No source found establishes “adjacent quanta execute one another” as a universal ontology.
- No source establishes that SCC/cycles alone imply positive survival probability.
- No source establishes that Lindblad jump rates produce independent Poisson offspring in an interacting, exclusion-limited network.
- No source establishes that nonselected quantum outcomes become dark matter or dark energy, or that they gravitate in the selected branch.
