# Alternative closure routes: quantum-neighbor bootstrap

Status: COMPLETE

The P0 findings are structural, so the routes below first change or narrow the
claim; tuning $\kappa$, $\gamma$, a seed, or a cosmic readout cannot repair
them.

## R1: local monitored microscopic model

Start with a local system--bath Hamiltonian, finite interaction range, bath
state, and weak-coupling/Markov/secular limit.  Derive (C1)--(C2), the energy
current into the bath/drive, and a concrete instrument for its records.
Choices are the factorization, detector, coarse graining and locality scale.
Falsify with a coherent $H$ control (diagonal closure must fail as predicted),
a no-drive energy accounting control, and an alternative unravelling of the
same Lindbladian.  A pass establishes the narrowly stated `enabled transition`
meaning, not a universal quantum ontology.  Degrees of freedom are the local
Hamiltonian/couplings and record coarse graining; it is target-aware only if
chosen after a desired cosmic result.

## R2: infinite-volume stochastic limit

Specify a sequence of graphs/cells, seed law, windowing rule, and scaling in
which exclusion, collision, common-bath and overlap errors vanish.  Prove a
coupling or quantitative bound to a multitype Galton--Watson process.  Matched
controls are an acyclic graph, a cyclic subcritical row mean, finite volumes
at increasing time, and a high-density collision regime.  Reject the route if
the error does not vanish or if survival disappears in the stated limit.
The free choices are graph sequence, scaling and time window; they must be
registered before inspecting an endpoint.  This route has no cosmological
observable by itself.

## R3: age-structured alternative

If physical parents have exponential lifetimes, retain their age/lifetime as a
state variable and use a Crump--Mode--Jagers or equivalent age-structured
branching model rather than independent Poisson generations.  Derive its
offspring kernel and Malthusian threshold, and compare it to the false
fixed-window Poisson reduction using equal mean rate.  The decisive falsifier
is the variance and cross-type covariance in (M8); fitting only the mean is
not sufficient.  This route changes the theorem and has at least the lifetime
law and age kernel as additional degrees of freedom.

## R4: declared CE scalar readout

After R1--R2 (or R3) establishes a particular stochastic object, separately
postulate or derive a map from its *instrument-defined nonselected records* to
the residual field.  The map must state scale, local/covariant kernel, stress
transfer, selected-branch rule and visible/environment no-double-counting.
The predecessor's two-outcome conditioning control is mandatory: ordinary
conditional quantum mechanics must not be relabelled as the new source rule.
Then test the residual scalar with a frozen perturbation and abundance forward
model.  Reject if the map, $M_*$, field amplitude or $V_\Lambda$ is retuned
after a failed observable.

## Decision

The allowed central statement is: *declared local facilitated transitions can
form mutually enabling support graphs; under an independently proven or
declared branching limit, a reachable supercritical component has nonzero
survival probability; an instrument-defined residual-sector physical map may
then be introduced as new physics.*  The following remain excluded by the
contract and M1--M6: automatic Poisson offspring, survival of a finite closed
network, energy creation by a neighbor, and automatic gravitational sourcing
by nonselected quantum paths.
