# Ranked independent route portfolio

Status: COMPLETE

## Locked common frame

Freeze the unit-to-chart map, $g_0$, anatomical covariates, one horizon, proper score, exclusions, and family rule before outcome access. Fit on earlier blocks, select once in discovery animals, and score once on temporally later held-out animals. Cell/window/path rows are nested observations, never independent samples. Every induced-field route compares $g_t$ with baseline $g_0$, baseline-null $g_t=g_0$, and the equally flexible pullback-null $f_t^*g_0$.

`H_W` requires a preregistered $\Phi(W^s,c)$ to predict independently estimated later deformation beyond surface geodesic, layer, cell type, wiring length, rate, gain, and noise. `H_G` requires later-path or first-passage proper-score gain over parameter-matched direct $v,Q$, Euclidean, gain-only and noise-only models. `H_C` requires both plus randomized/validly instrumented $W^s$ change and mediation retaining a direct structural-to-path route.

No $H_C$ label is eligible unless $\Phi(W^s,c)$, the $g\to(v,Q)$ bridge,
its parameter budget, the unconstrained $v,Q$ alternative, and a direct
$W^s\to$ path term are frozen before outcome access. A3 and A4 are Tier A only
within their declared $H_G$ scope and are not full-chain experiments.

Baseline producers are separate locked candidates: `P-h` from physical
surface/laminar anatomy, `P-W` from pre-existing structural connectivity,
`P-D` from pre-period dynamics, and `P-C` from their declared combination.
Select them only with pre-intervention calibration, estimate relative
deformation against each retained producer, and require both a pre-period
pseudo-intervention placebo and a matched flat-pullback null.

## Exact route conditions

### A1: smallest proposed full-chain design under stated assumptions

First measure surface geometry $h$, the four locked $g_0$ producer candidates,
cell identities and synapses in a fixed population. Randomize a target-specific
plasticity intervention and sham at animal or independently replicated circuit
level. Include negative-target, off-target and matched-rate controls; otherwise
use a valid instrument with explicit exclusion assumptions and sensitivity
bounds. Re-measure $W^s$; estimate $g_t$ from later evoked perturbation trials
not used to fit $\Phi$; finally collect a later spontaneous activity/behavior
block. Score predeclared $\Phi(W^s,c)\to L_t$ with SPD loss, then score a
declared stochastic bridge against direct $v,Q$, retaining the direct
$W^s\to$ path term in mediation. Include h-geodesic, layer, cell class, wiring
length and stimulation spread in both models. This is the smallest *proposed*
full-chain design relative to $H_C$, not an unconditional technological
minimum. It loses if any link or exclusion check fails, or a physical/
developmental baseline explains the outcome equally well.

### A2: causal perturbation

Inputs are randomized intervention $Z$, target $W^s$, evoked/spontaneous activity, and behavior. Estimate $Z\to\Delta g$ before looking at later trajectories. In mediation retain $Z\to x$ and pre-treatment covariates; broad stimulation is ineligible for structural mediation unless matched-rate and off-target controls demonstrate exclusion. Use animal-level randomization inference.

### A3: local response geometry

At registered $z,c$, randomize small calibrated input directions through measured $B$. Estimate susceptibility or response ellipsoids from trials separate from later outcome trials. Hold out input directions and animals. The decisive comparison is response bridge versus spontaneous covariance, direct dynamics and gain/noise, with equal parameter budgets.

### A4: control geometry

The control object is $W_{C,H}=\sum\Psi BR_u^{-1}B^\top\Psi^\top$, not passive $Q$. Predetermine targets and score actual energy plus success probability. If $B$ or $R_u$ is unmeasured, report the route ineligible instead of substituting $Q$.

### A5: sleep and replay

Randomize sleep/replay manipulation, use wake, circadian, arousal and replay-matched controls, and measure pre/post structure plus later paths. A Ricci-flow interpretation is eligible only after a field-evolution law is declared and beats matched smoothing/denoising nulls.

## Feasibility ranking and credibility loss

1. A3 is the fastest high-information precursor, but only tests $H_G$.
2. A1 is the smallest proposed full-chain design under its measurement,
   target-specificity, exclusion, and mediation assumptions.
3. A2 becomes most decisive if target specificity and exclusion are demonstrated.
4. A5 should follow A1/A3 for the sleep theory.
5. A4 and transition/curvature routes are sharp but require calibrated input, dense sampling, or declared boundaries.
6. Cross-modal, behavioral, public-data, and synthetic routes improve transferability or power but cannot replace A1/A2.

The specified bridge loses credibility if preregistered A1/A2 repeatedly shows no stable $\Delta W^s\to L_t$, only gain/noise effects, no proper-score gain over direct dynamics, equal performance after cell permutation or baseline/pullback replacement, or incompatible effect direction across independent animals. This refutes the named connectivity-mediated bridge, not every possible neural geometry.
