## Verification notes

- E01–E22 resolve to official Nature/Springer, Oxford Academic, or DOI records. E11’s DOI landing page was access-limited in this run, so metadata beyond the DOI/title is marked conservatively; do not treat it as independently verified for sample-size extraction until the Science record/supplement is opened.
- E05 and E14 are official Oxford Academic records; the article abstract endpoint was intermittently unavailable, so exact cohort preprocessing and uncertainty must be taken from the full record before quantitative reuse.
- None of the 22 records directly measures all three links ΔW→Δg→Δx(t). E13/E15 provide the strongest same-time-window candidates for a new branching/trajectory analysis; E01/E02 provide neural subspace/geometry endpoints without longitudinal structural W; E18 provides causal timing but not W.
- Therefore these sources support empirical subclaims only. They do not establish a universal neural metric, a cache/unit-test algorithm, or an AGI bridge.
