# Alternative closure routes: cosmology--quantum mechanics

Status: COMPLETE

## Route R1 — microscopic instrument to count process

**Goal.** Derive a physically selected quantum instrument and its classical
counting/unravelling limit from a declared CE+matter action, rather than
starting from supplied jump operators.

**Fixed inputs and free structure.** Fix the microscopic action, state,
system--environment factorization, gauge treatment, detector coupling,
coarse-graining scale, and outcome algebra before fitting any cosmological
quantity.  The allowed derived objects are the CPTP instrument, Lindblad or
Davies generator (if its limit applies), and count-process law; the type basis,
unravelling, and rate matrix may not be selected after inspecting $\Omega_i$.

**Degrees of freedom / target awareness.** The present CE values
$q\simeq\Omega_b$ and the density ratios are already visible targets, so this
route is target-aware.  At minimum it introduces a system--bath split,
couplings/spectral density, preparation, coarse-graining time, and outcome
algebra; no numerical comparison can be counted as blind unless these are
preregistered.

**Falsifiers and controls.** Reject if complete positivity, Born consistency,
basis robustness, or the claimed Markov/secular approximation fails.  Compare
against coherent-Hamiltonian and collective-jump controls, which must fail
population closure; test independent increments and genealogy directly rather
than infer them from nonnegative rates.  A successful supplied-generator test
is only a conditional structural result, not a pass for this route.

## Route R2 — covariant residual-sector EFT

**Goal.** Turn the nonselected measure into a local covariant effective field
theory, with a defined transition hypersurface and stress tensor.

**Fixed inputs and free structure.** Fix a Lorentz/diffeomorphism-covariant
action $S[g,\phi,\ldots]$, field content, renormalization prescription,
initial state, and the local map from histories to fields.  Derive
$T_{\mu\nu}$ by metric variation and state whether interactions exchange
energy with visible species.  Kernel and prior selection are not permitted to
depend on the desired density fractions.

**Degrees of freedom / target awareness.** This route adds field content,
potential/couplings, initial data, hypersurface prescription, and
renormalization conditions.  It is target-aware because the existing dark
sector fractions motivate it; those parameters must be constrained by
non-cosmological or held-out observables before density fitting.

**Falsifiers and controls.** Reject if the proposed map is foliation- or
coordinate-dependent, the stress tensor is nonconserved absent a declared
exchange current, ghosts/gradient instabilities occur, or the local EFT cannot
reproduce the proposed pushforward in its stated limit.  A deliberately
nonlocal global-path kernel is the matched negative control: it can define the
integral but must not pass locality/covariance.

## Route R3 — species-current to observational forward model

**Goal.** Map any covariant residual EFT to baryon, dark-matter, and vacuum
components using conserved currents and a forward model through background,
perturbations, recombination, and detector likelihoods.

**Fixed inputs and free structure.** Before data comparison fix the transition
hypersurface, $\rho_{\rm crit}$ convention, species-current matching,
interaction terms, initial perturbation spectrum, nuisance model, data split,
and likelihood.  Solve Einstein--Boltzmann dynamics and publish all resulting
observables, not only the three density fractions.

**Degrees of freedom / target awareness.** The choice $\Omega_b=q$ and ratio
$\Omega_c/\Omega_\Lambda=\alpha_sD$ is explicitly target-aware, with at least
two independent readout choices plus cosmological initial/nuisance parameters.
Look-elsewhere accounting must include alternative partitions and data
combinations.  The current ratio diagnostic has no qualifying independent
holdout, so it cannot validate this route.

**Falsifiers and controls.** Reject if the model violates total
$\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu}=0$, fails CMB/BAO/SN/growth likelihoods under the
fixed protocol, or only succeeds after retuning the readout.  Compare with
flat $\Lambda$CDM and with same-parameter alternative dark-ratio partitions;
the latter tests whether apparent agreement comes from the fixed point or the
chosen readout.

## Decision

The routes are sequential, not substitutes: R1 establishes a quantum count
interpretation; R2 establishes covariant gravitational sourcing; R3 establishes
a species/observable readout.  Until all required falsifiers are passed under
fixed inputs, the present result remains a conditional mathematical fixed point
plus incomplete/axiomatic physical bridges.
