# CE cosmology--quantum mechanics audit contract

Status: COMPLETE

PREDECESSOR: none

## 1. Question and scope

Audit the present CE chain

$$
\text{environmental selection}
\longrightarrow \text{folded/nonselected sector}
\longrightarrow \text{cosmological dark-sector readout}
$$

with particular attention to the seam between open quantum dynamics and
cosmology. The audit asks which links are definitions, conditional theorems,
adopted axioms, numerical outputs, empirical formulae, or incomplete physical
maps; whether the mathematics used at each closed link is correct; and whether
current observational comparisons support more than a neutral consistency
statement.

This run is read-only with respect to canonical CE theory and code. It may
produce only the numbered research-stage files and supporting artifacts in
this run. Any later correction to a canonical ledger or narrative requires a
separately authorized implementation step with the appropriate writer role.

## 3. Registered claims

### Q1. Quantum selection

Determine whether the repository derives a specific quantum instrument or
unravelling, outcome rule, and Born-consistent selection dynamics from a
declared microscopic action. If any of these are absent, the selection link
remains incomplete even when a supplied generator passes numerical checks.

### Q2. Folded-sector preservation

Determine whether a nonselected subprobability measure and its kernel
pushforward are mathematically well-defined, and separately whether the
resulting object is shown to be a local covariant quantum field with a
conserved stress tensor. Measure-theoretic closure does not imply the latter.

### Q3. Cosmological readout

Verify the conditional Poisson fixed-point calculation and the status of the
maps from its dimensionless outputs to $\Omega_b$, $\Omega_{\rm DM}$, and
$\Omega_\Lambda$. A physically derived readout requires a microscopic action,
covariant transition hypersurface, conserved species currents, total
stress-energy conservation, and a forward model through perturbations and
observables.

### Q4. Observational status

Check current primary-source values only to assess numerical proximity and
compatibility. Unless the data, likelihood, nuisance treatment, and tested
observable were fixed independently of the CE construction, proximity is not
a prediction or evidence of derivation.

## 4. Falsifiers and decision rule

A claimed theorem is narrowed or rejected if a missing premise, counterexample,
dimension error, inconsistent status across canonical documents, or failed
focused regression is found. A bridge is closed only if the required physical
map is explicitly defined and its conservation, covariance, and observable
consequences follow under stated assumptions. Otherwise it remains
`[미완성]` or `[공리]` as appropriate.

The claim ceiling is a formal status audit of the current CE corpus. This run
cannot establish that CE is a correct theory of measurement, quantum gravity,
dark matter, dark energy, inflation, or observed cosmology.

## 5. Lanes and validation

The physics-source lane checks primary sources and current observational
inputs. The mathematics lane checks derivations, dimensions, counterexamples,
and the narrow local tests. A status auditor then compares both lanes against
the frozen corpus. No implementation or full test suite is authorized.
