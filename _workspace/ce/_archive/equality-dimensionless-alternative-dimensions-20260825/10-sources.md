## Guardrails

* $d$ is spatial dimension, $D$ spacetime dimension, and $p$ form degree. Compact/internal and effective/spectral dimensions are distinct.
* “Critical dimension” is a model consistency condition, not an empirical measurement.
* No source supports “dark matter/dark energy is an observed extra-dimensional sector.”
