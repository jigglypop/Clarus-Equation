## Route-specific boundary cases

1. Hodge: establish orientation, nondegenerate metric, dimension and signature
   first.  In Lorentzian 3+1, real 2-form self-duality is not available under
   the stated convention because `star^2=-1`; a complex self-duality condition
   is a different construction.
2. Binomial: enumerate integer `0<=p,q<=n`; outside this discrete domain the
   elementary strict-unimodality proof is inapplicable.
3. Cross product: use the norm/orthogonality definition.  A looser bilinear
   antisymmetric product would be a different problem and does not have the
   `0,1,3,7` classification.
4. Field dimensions: report whether `p` counts a potential or field strength.
   Treat D=2 and D=3 Einstein gravity as special boundary cases, not ordinary
   substitutions into a D>=4 graviton formula.

## Required bridge before an existence claim

For an extra-dimensional candidate, freeze a model family before inspecting
the result: geometry/topology, compactification scale(s), field localization,
observables, likelihood/data set and matched four-dimensional control.  The
experiment must distinguish the model from the control at a declared
sensitivity.  Existing null searches constrain specified parameter regions;
they are neither a no-go for every compactification nor confirmation of extra
dimensions.  No current CE quantity supplies this bridge.
