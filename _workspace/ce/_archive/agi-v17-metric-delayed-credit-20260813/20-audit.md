## 3. Hidden-premise and counterexample audit

N1 genuinely needs full $GL(d)$ and specifically $-I$. A chart subgroup that
excludes the inversion may carry orientation and is not covered. Likewise,
covariance of an averaged stochastic kernel does not imply pathwise equality:
the lane's $d=1$ random signed-exponent construction is a valid counterexample
to that stronger inference. D2 excludes it by requiring covariance for almost
every fixed seed.

N2 and N3 need joint, not merely componentwise, independence of the complete
seed/state/topology tuple from $S$. They also need the same paired realization.
A sign-correlated joint seed, retained prior seed, agent role, raw cue message,
or oriented private anchor is a side channel and lies outside the theorem. The
ordering clause in D5 is essential: if a component may transmit raw $x=su$
along with public $u$ before the sign-blind update, it can directly recover the
sign. Permutation equivariance alone neither removes such a channel nor
constructs a countable limit.

The countable extension is valid because equality holds coordinatewise under
one coupling and measurable kernels preserve equality of laws. Projective
compatibility is not automatic for an unrelated family of finite-$N$ systems.
Nor does equality at every finite depth define an infinite-depth output unless
a limit or whole-trajectory functional is supplied. These regularity clauses
are theorem premises and must remain visible in any later documentation.

N4 is exactly a two-class conditional information separation. Isotropically
randomized $U$ can make $I(S;G_T)=0$ while $(G_T,U)$ still decodes $S$.
Conversely, the one-bit bound neither proves that one additional real scalar is
necessary nor limits exact-real SPD capacity: without continuity, finite
precision or noise assumptions, a real entry can pathologically encode much
more information.

H1 avoids N1 only by adding the homogeneous splitting. The augmented cues
$(u,1)$ and $(-u,1)$ are not global negatives in the augmented space. The
spatial--homogeneous cross block is a cue-odd covector, and the final scalar is
an anchor self-coupling. Thus one `SPD(d+1)` or one Cholesky factor is one
storage field but is not strict D1 metric-only memory. At $d=3$ it has 10
independent real coordinates instead of 6: exactly four additional ambient
coordinates. The eligibility route is smaller at nine coordinates, so the
homogeneous route has no information-theoretic or coordinate-minimality claim.

The public terminal reference $u$ is also essential. H1 remembers one sign
relative to that known reference; it does not answer an unknown later query
direction. Its analytic immediate write is a narrow delayed-memory primitive,
not learned causal attribution from a delayed reward.

## 4. Route and implementation authorization

Implementation is authorized only for R-H with R-S and R-N as no-go controls.
R-E, R-F and R-A remain disclosed comparators, not alternative ways to call an
original-space metric sign-sensitive. In particular, R-A must retain its
oriented anchor in the semantic-state ledger and cannot claim strict D1.

The implementation and evaluator must enforce all of the following:

- persist exactly one positive canonical factor for $G\in\operatorname{SPD}(4)$
  in the lift, with no cached cue, optimizer, eligibility vector, role bit, or
  prior random seed; public $u$ is a transient input supplied again at readout;
- report all 10 factor coordinates and the +4 coordinate delta, rather than
  hiding the covector/scalar block behind the phrase "one field";
- use the frozen embeddings, $\eta=1$ and $c=4$, and independently compute the
  reference costs and decisions in the evaluator;
- transport with $A=\operatorname{diag}(J,1)$, including the initial metric;
  resetting it to identity, normalizing $Ju$, reprojecting, or claiming general
  $GL(4)$ semantics fails H1-C;
- make the strict $+u/-u$ snapshots byte-identical, and make the action law
  identical by construction, on every paired confirmation fixture;
- test only the registered finite ensemble sizes
  $N\in\{1,2,4,8,16,64\}$ numerically. Those scores do not empirically verify a
  countable or infinite-depth system; those conclusions remain mathematical
  and conditional as stated above;
- include axis/near-axis cues, both signs, chart singular-value endpoints,
  exact snapshot round trips, nonfinite/zero rejection, persistent-field
  introspection, and the homogeneous-coordinate deletion killing test;
- seal the final contract, production module, public export, independent
  evaluator, development result and every threshold before opening seed
  1,720,000. The exclusive receipt and result must make any second opening
  fail closed, and any bound-file change requires a new run and seed block.

Passing development arithmetic or a subset of these tests is insufficient.
The two positive decision labels remain conditional on their complete
