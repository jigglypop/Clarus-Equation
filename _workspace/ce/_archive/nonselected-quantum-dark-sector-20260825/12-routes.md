# Alternative closure routes: nonselected quantum paths as a dark sector

Status: COMPLETE

The P0 finding in M5 activates these routes.  They are sequential: a later
route cannot repair a missing earlier physical map by fitting cosmological
fractions.

## R1: microscopic instrument and conditional-gravity rule

Fix, before cosmological comparison, a microscopic action, system/environment
split, detector coupling, outcome algebra, initial state, and the proposed
gravity coupling.  Derive the quantum instrument and state whether gravity is
sourced by a conditional state, an ensemble state, or a new residual degree
of freedom.  The target is a covariant conserved source, not merely a
nonselected probability.  Free choices include the factorization, apparatus,
coarse graining, and gravity rule; this route is target-aware because the
observed dark sector motivated it.  Falsify it with the two-outcome
conditioning control in M5, Born/instrument consistency, and a no-signalling
or branch-conditioning test.  A pass must show exactly where the extra source
enters and where compensating energy resides.

## R2: covariant residual EFT and transition matching

Adopt C1 only with a local-covariant kernel, real integrable field, physical
scale $M_*$, and a specified transition hypersurface.  Couple the resulting
field to C2 and derive its metric stress tensor.  Fix the potential,
renormalization prescription, initial data, and any transfer current before
using $\Omega$ data.  Required controls are a deliberately nonlocal kernel
(must fail locality), a nonconserved transition (must fail M3), and an
explicit visible-plus-residual accounting check (must prevent double counting).
The smallest successful claim is the M6 common-origin axiom plus conditional
E1--E3, not an inference from decoherence.

## R3: perturbations and observational forward model

Fix $m$, self-interactions, initial perturbations, the data split, nuisance
model, and likelihood before comparison.  Evolve the full Einstein--Boltzmann
system through CMB, BAO, lensing, growth, and small-scale clustering.  The
mode condition $k\ll k_J$ must be checked over the claimed CDM domain rather
than inferred from the homogeneous average.  Compare against flat Lambda-CDM
and an equal-flexibility scalar-residual control.  Reject if the model needs
post-hoc retuning of $M_*$, amplitude, $V_\Lambda$, or the readout after a
failed observable.

## R4: independent abundance closure

Derive or independently constrain $M_*$, $m$, the initial field amplitude,
and $V_\Lambda$ without setting them from the observed fractions.  Then use a
frozen transfer prescription to obtain the absolute present densities.  The
matched null control is the same C2 dynamics with a different allowed
amplitude or constant offset: it demonstrates the continuum of abundances
that C1 and the Poisson root leave free.  Until this route passes a held-out
observable, any numerical agreement in $\Omega_i$ remains target-aware
calibration, not a prediction.

## Decision

R1 or R2 can retain the user's intended central idea in its honest form: dark
matter-like and dark-energy-like behavior are two regimes of a *new,
explicitly postulated residual sector* assigned a common nonselected-history
origin.  No route permits the stronger claim that ordinary unselected quantum
paths automatically gravitate in the selected branch.  Reopening that claim
requires a microscopic, empirically testable modification of the
quantum-gravity/source rule that passes R1--R3.
