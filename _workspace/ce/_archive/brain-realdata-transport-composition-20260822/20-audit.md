# Preimplementation audit

Status: COMPLETE

Gate: PASS

The input is a verified local copy of an official real dataset. The three
windows are equal-lag and lie after cue but before Go. Whole trials, not
frames, are held out. Every chart and affine map is train-fold only. The
direct two-step operator, persistence, derangement, coordinate-permutation,
stationary, and reverse controls are executable with identical latent rank.
No target label, behavioral outcome, curvature, graph support, or endpoint is
used. The contract explicitly prevents upgrading observational predictive
closure to synaptic, anatomical, consolidation, or causal evidence.

Pre-endpoint focused-test revision: an independent-phase null can beat
persistence merely by shrinking toward the train mean. The decision therefore
also requires positive held-out skill against the train-fold mean. This closes
the mean-regression loophole before any real-data score is opened.
