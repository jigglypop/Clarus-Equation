# Alternative recurrent-memory route decision

Status: COMPLETE

Overall verdict: **STOP for every registered successor claim.**

The campaign changed mechanisms rather than repeatedly tuning the failed native STDP route. It
tested frozen-M1 factor transfer, lagged contrastive learning, a frozen native-state predictor, and
teacher-forced replay-residual writing. All integrity and leakage barriers held, but none cleared its
registered causal/generalization gate.

The most informative positive capability is narrow: continuous target replay plus a residual-credit
outer product can install robust cue-value binding in the native recurrent weight and retain it after
all external stores are physically removed. That capability is not evidence for the proposed
predictive timing rule, because a fixed cyclic mismatch of the three residual-credit pairs performs
equally on binding and substantially better on held-out factor transfer.

The strongest negative conclusions are:

1. confirmed pairwise binding does not automatically yield compositional transfer;
2. a reset-to-zero negative phase contributes no contrastive signal;
3. the frozen linear native-state predictor does not beat persistence under the registered OOD test;
4. teacher-forced recurrent writing is real, but its success does not identify precise temporal
   prediction-error credit assignment.

Confirmation seeds remain unopened. No biological consolidation, hippocampal-cortical transfer,
abstract reasoning, self-model, or consciousness claim is supported. The next independent question
is whether direct recurrent-weight intervention creates a separately calibrated geometric mediator
with unique held-out dynamical value.
