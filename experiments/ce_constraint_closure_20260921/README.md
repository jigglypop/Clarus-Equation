# CE-CC1: inverse closure and the sign of the metric kinetic term

The analytic derivation is in
[chapter 35](../../paper/후속연구_기록과_상태선택/35_제약대수에서_Einstein_작용과_공통_전파원뿔까지.md).

For the stated ultralocal quadratic pure-metric Hamiltonian with a potential
linear in spatial curvature, the Lorentzian hypersurface-deformation algebra
fixes the trace coefficient to 1/2 and the product of kinetic and curvature
coefficients to -1. The Einstein action then follows by a Legendre transform.
The required metric kinetic form has five positive and one negative directions.

A positive-kinetic example on a flat compact torus satisfies H=0 and D_i=0
pointwise but has a nonzero Hamiltonian-constraint bracket, with two strictly
positive lapse functions. A minimal sigma model closes with the same spacetime
algebra only when its momentum and spatial-gradient matrices are inverses.

The algebra and counterexample integrals are exact. This is a restricted
application of established geometrodynamics/constraint methods, not a new general
uniqueness theorem. The origin of the Lorentzian deformation algebra, quantum
constraint closure, constants, additional constraints, nonminimal scalar-tensor
terms and higher derivatives remain outside this derivation.
